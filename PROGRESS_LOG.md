## Week 1 – Choosing my topic and starting data collection

**Date: 10 October 2026**

I decided to explore the question **“Why do I listen to music?”** for my Dear Data project.

I chose this topic because music is something I listen to almost every day, but I realised that I listen to different genres depending on my mood, time of day and what I am doing.

For example, I often listen to pop music when I am getting ready in the morning because it makes me feel happy and motivated. I also listen to Bollywood music when I get home in the afternoon.

I started collecting my observations by recording each separate listening session.

The information I decided to collect includes:
- Date of listening session
- Time of day
- Music genre
- My mood
- Duration of listening
- What I was doing at the time

**Date: 12 October 2026**

I created my first CSV file called `data.csv` to organise my observations.

I decided that one observation would represent one separate listening session rather than every individual song. This makes it easier to compare how often I listen to music and identify patterns.

I also started using different categories for mood, including happy, relaxed, motivated, bored and emotional.
